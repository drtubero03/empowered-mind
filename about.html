About Dr. Adrian Tubero

Licensed Psychologist • Founder of Empowered Mind

Bio

For people who understand themselves — but still feel stuck.

I'm Dr. Adrian Tubero, a licensed psychologist, founder of Empowered Mind, and have been in private practice for over 20 years.

My work is depth-oriented, experiential, and deeply relational — designed for thoughtful, self-aware people who understand themselves intellectually, yet still find themselves stuck in familiar patterns, relationship struggles, or the quiet feeling that something important hasn't fully shifted.

I'm not a silent participant in the room. Therapy with me is active, collaborative, and deeply engaged. I help people make sense of experiences that can feel confusing, painful, or hard to hold alone. Together, we slow things down, get underneath familiar patterns, and make space for what's been difficult to understand, feel, or change.

Many of the people I work with have already done therapy, reflected deeply, read books, and spent years trying to understand themselves. What they're often longing for is not more insight alone, but a different kind of experience — one that feels emotionally honest, deeply supportive, and capable of creating real movement.

My approach is warm, direct, and emotionally attuned. I care deeply about helping people feel understood, supported, and held as they move through some of the most meaningful and challenging seasons of their lives.

Much of my work is with individuals navigating midlife — identity shifts, relationship transitions, grief, ambition, meaning, and the quiet question of what comes next.

A significant part of my work draws from Core Self Reclamation Therapy (CSRT), an experiential model developed by SueAnne Piliero, with whom I've trained extensively under direct mentorship. I integrate this work into an individualized therapeutic process that is emotionally attuned, depth-oriented, and tailored to what each person most needs.

I also offer The Midlife Shift — a small experiential group for women navigating transition, identity shifts, and the emotional realities of midlife.

Approach

How the work moves.

Experiential

Change happens through lived emotional experience — not insight alone.

Depth-Oriented

We go to the root of what's keeping you stuck, not just the surface symptoms.

Focused & Intentional

Some clients come for focused work around a particular transition or struggle. Others stay longer as deeper layers emerge. Together, we determine the pace, depth, and shape of the work.
