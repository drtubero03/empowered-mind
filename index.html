The work

A Different Way of Working

This is intentional, depth-oriented work that goes to the root of what's keeping you stuck — so real change can happen. Rather than circling the same insight, we work directly with the parts of you that hold these patterns in place.

I draw on a range of experiential and depth-oriented approaches, tailored to what each person actually needs. The goal isn't more insight — it's change at the level where it actually lives. So you don't just understand yourself better — you begin to experience yourself differently.

About Dr. Tubero

Hi, I'm Dr. Adrian Tubero.

I'm a psychologist and founder of Empowered Mind. My work is depth-oriented and experiential — built around the understanding that insight, on its own, is rarely enough to create the change you're looking for.

Many of the people I work with have already done significant personal work. They've been in therapy, understood their patterns, and can speak clearly about where they came from. What they're often looking for is something that allows that understanding to finally become lived — not just known.

Much of my work is with individuals navigating midlife — identity shifts, relationship transitions, grief, ambition, meaning, and the quiet question of what comes next.

Scope

What I Work With

I work with adults who are:

  • Struggling with long-standing emotional patterns
  • Navigating anxiety, burnout, identity shifts, or midlife transitions
  • Emotionally intelligent and self-aware — but still stuck
  • Ready to go beyond insight into felt, lasting change

I offer:

  • Ongoing individual psychotherapy
  • Focused, time-limited work for specific concerns
  • Periodic check-in sessions for returning clients

I do not offer:

  • Couples counseling
  • Substance abuse treatment
  • Crisis intervention
While I don't offer couples therapy, I do work deeply with relationship dynamics — including attachment wounds, betrayal, disconnection, and trust repair.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

This work is for self-aware, motivated adults who feel stuck despite having done significant personal work. This work is especially well-suited for those who feel they've plateaued in more insight-oriented therapy — or sense there is something deeper that conversation alone has not fully reached.

Traditional talk therapy often works primarily at the cognitive level — understanding what happened and why.

This work goes deeper. Drawing on experiential, somatic, and depth-oriented approaches such as AEDP and CSRT, we work at the emotional and embodied level where long-standing patterns actually live.

Insight matters — but meaningful change happens through experience, not explanation. The goal is not simply to understand yourself differently, but to begin experiencing yourself differently.

AEDP and CSRT are experiential, depth-oriented therapeutic approaches that help access the emotional patterns living underneath symptoms, stuckness, and repeating dynamics.

Rather than working only through insight or conversation, these approaches help create change through emotional experience, nervous system regulation, and deeper self-connection.

I thoughtfully integrate these approaches depending on each person's needs — there is no one-size-fits-all model.

I do not accept insurance, though superbills are available for those seeking out-of-network reimbursement through their provider. Session fees and format details are outlined on the How I Work page, and we can also discuss any further questions during our consultation call.

It depends entirely on what you're working on and what you need. Some people do focused, time-limited work around a specific concern. Others engage in ongoing depth therapy over a longer arc. We discuss this in our initial consultation and revisit it as the work evolves — there's no one-size-fits-all structure.

After you submit the inquiry form, I'll review it and reach out within a few business days to schedule a brief consultation call. That conversation gives us both a chance to get a sense of fit — what you're looking for, whether this approach makes sense for where you are, and what working together might look like.
