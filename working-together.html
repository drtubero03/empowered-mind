The Work

How I Work

My practice is grounded in experiential and attachment-based approaches, including Core Self Reclamation Therapy (CSRT), AEDP, psychodynamic, and emotionally focused therapies. I draw on these frameworks not as protocols, but as tools — shaped by what each person needs.

Most people who seek this kind of work are already self-aware. They've often been in therapy before. They understand their patterns intellectually. And something still isn't moving.

That gap — between understanding and genuine change — is where this work begins.

How the Work Moves

Three qualities that define this approach

Experiential

Change happens not only through insight, but through felt emotional experience — where longstanding patterns actually live. Sessions are designed to move beneath the narrative to what's held in the body and emotional memory.

Depth-Oriented

We work beneath symptoms to understand and shift the emotional patterns underneath — including attachment wounds, early relational dynamics, and the parts of you that haven't yet been reached by more surface-level work.

Focused & Intentional

Some clients come for shorter-term, focused work around a specific transition or pattern. Others stay longer as deeper layers emerge. Together, we determine the pace and depth that feels most useful.

A Good Fit

Who This Work Is For — and Who It's Not

This work tends to be a good fit for:

  • High-functioning, self-aware adults who want real change — not more explanation
  • Those who have hit a ceiling in previous therapy and want a different depth of work
  • Adults navigating midlife transitions — relational, professional, or identity-related
  • People ready to work experientially, not only cognitively
  • Those who can commit to consistent, sustained engagement

For women navigating the specific terrain of midlife, I also offer The Midlife Shift →

This may not be the right fit if:

  • You are in active crisis requiring intensive or emergency intervention
  • You are seeking couples therapy or substance use treatment
  • You prefer a primarily directive or psychoeducational approach
I do not offer couples therapy — but I work deeply with relational dynamics, attachment wounds, betrayal, and trust repair within individual therapy.

If you are in crisis, please call or text 988 or visit your nearest emergency room.
