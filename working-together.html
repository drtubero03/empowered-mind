Process

How I Work

Therapy with me is collaborative, emotionally engaged, and tailored to the person sitting in front of me. Some people come for support around a particular transition or struggle; others stay longer as deeper layers emerge.

Three Qualities

How the Work Moves

Experiential

Change happens not only through insight, but through lived emotional experience — where patterns are felt, understood, and gently transformed.

Depth-Oriented

We work beneath surface symptoms to understand the emotional patterns underneath — attachment wounds, relational dynamics, protective strategies, and parts of yourself still organized around old experiences.

Individualized

Some people come for focused work around a particular transition or struggle. Others stay longer as deeper layers emerge. Together, we determine the pace, depth, and direction of the work.

What We Can Work Through

You may recognize yourself here.

People come to this work carrying many different things. What they share is a sense that something is stuck — and a readiness to work at depth.

Fit

Is This Work a Good Fit?

This work tends to resonate with thoughtful, emotionally curious adults who are looking for something deeper than insight alone.

  • You've done therapy before but something important still hasn't shifted
  • You're navigating identity shifts, grief, relational challenges, burnout, or transitions in midlife
  • You're interested in understanding yourself more deeply — emotionally, relationally, and experientially
  • You're looking for a therapy relationship that is engaged, thoughtful, and depth-oriented

At this time, I do not offer couples therapy or intensive crisis treatment. If you are in immediate crisis, please call or text 988 or seek emergency support.

Fees & Format

Working Together

Sessions are typically 45 minutes, though some people benefit from 60-minute sessions depending on the work and clinical need.

Appointments are available virtually via secure telehealth, and in person in Great Neck, NY — on the North Shore of Long Island, approximately 30 minutes from Penn Station via the Long Island Railroad.

This is a private-pay practice. Sessions typically range from $350–$375 depending on session length and the nature of the work. Superbills are available for those seeking potential out-of-network reimbursement through insurance.
