Process

How I Work

Therapy with me is collaborative, emotionally engaged, and deeply tailored to the person sitting in front of me. Some people come because something feels stuck. Others are navigating a rupture, transition, grief, or a quiet sense that something important needs to change.

We move at the pace of what feels meaningful — thoughtfully, relationally, and with depth.

Therapy that feels different.

This work is not only about understanding yourself intellectually. It is about slowing down enough to notice what is happening underneath — emotionally, relationally, and internally — so change can begin to feel real in your daily life.

The Process

How the Work Moves

Experiential

Lasting change happens through emotionally lived experience — not simply understanding why something is happening.

Depth-Oriented

We work beneath surface symptoms to understand what is shaping your experience — old wounds, relationship patterns, protective ways of coping, and the parts of you still organized around earlier experiences.

Individualized

Some people come for focused work around a particular transition or struggle. Others stay longer as deeper layers emerge. Together, we determine the pace, depth, and shape of the work.

Fit

Is This Work a Good Fit?

This work tends to resonate with thoughtful, self-aware adults who are seeking deeper emotional change.

  • You've done therapy before but something important still hasn't shifted
  • You're navigating identity shifts, grief, relational challenges, burnout, or transitions in midlife
  • You're interested in understanding yourself more deeply — emotionally, relationally, and experientially
  • You're looking for a therapy relationship that is engaged, thoughtful, and depth-oriented

At this time, I do not offer couples therapy or intensive crisis treatment. If you are in immediate crisis, please call or text 988 or seek emergency support.
